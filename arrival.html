Eastern Asia Tips and Info

Before Flying

  • Depending on the country you are visiting, you can do arrival procedures online (Visit Japan) or obtain an E-KTA (South Korea).
  • Make sure to check online for any Visa requirements for the country you are visiting.
  • Your passport must not be expiring within six months before departure.
  • Countries in Eastern Asia have really good public transportation, it may be a good idea to research your route and either take a screenshot or print it out.
  • It's recommended to have money of the country's local currency before leaving.

When Arriving at the Airport

  • To ensure that you go to the right place, it is recommended to follow those that know their way to customs and immigration.
  • If you have not applied for the E-KTA or similar, make sure that the required forms are filled out to ensure a smooth process.
  • Depending on the country you are visiting, it may take long to inspect/declare items.

After Going Through Customs

  • After going through customs, acquire any SIM cards or other items that you may have ordered prior to leaving the airport.
  • If you require a service to take your luggage to your hotel, there are such services at the airport you are in to transport it for yoou for a fee.
  • Grab a transportation card and load it with money to begin your journey. They can be also used in participating convenience stores, vending machines (in Japan, South Korea is unknown) and restaurants.
  • Research train routes, depending on the city you're located in there can be a lot of lines in one station that can create confusion.
