Eastern Asia Tips and Info

Obtaining Local Currency

  • Depending on country you are visiting, International ATM's are available in Airports as well as train stations.
  • Option to withdraw money using Dynamic Conversion Rate or local currency.
  • A coin bag is necessary due to the large amount of coins you'll be carrying.
  • Avoiding foreign fees.
  • You can learn more about International ATM's with SevenBank in Japan at //www.sevenbank.co.jp/intlcard/index2.html or The Seoul Guide at https://www.theseoulguide.com/money/ for South Korean International ATM.
